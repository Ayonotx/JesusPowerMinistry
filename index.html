Welcome

A place to be trained, and a place to be still.

Jesus Power is a registered retreat center and training institution in Dawhenya, Ghana. We host personal retreats, church camps, conferences and weddings — and we train ministers through the Jesus Power School of Ministry.

Our programmes are delivered in partnership with Global University (Berean School of the Bible, Springfield, Missouri), alongside a hands-on certificate in practical ministry and leadership.
