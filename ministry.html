Welcome to JPSOM

Training for the work, not just the certificate

The Jesus Power School of Ministry (JPSOM) opened its doors on 24 April 2024, welcoming its first cohort with a mission to raise, equip and empower believers for impactful Christian ministry.

Founded through the vision God placed in the heart of Apostle Bismark Osei Akomeah, JPSOM provides accessible, practical and biblically sound training for Christians from all walks of life. Whether you have a strong academic background or none at all, our goal is to help you discover, develop and fulfil God's calling.

We believe every believer with a passion for serving God deserves quality ministerial training, mentorship and spiritual development in preparation for effective Kingdom service.

  • Dean of School of Ministry: Rev. Dr. Ralph Adjaho
  • Founded 24 April 2024 by Apostle Bismark Osei Akomeah
  • Two programmes: certificate and diploma
  • Hybrid learning — virtual and in-person classes

Library

Apostle Dr. Jones Boateng Library

Commissioned on 24 April 2024 · Librarian: Min. Rebecca Nuamah

The library was named after Apostle Bismark Akomeah's close friend, who has been a backbone and huge support to this project. It houses over 3,000 Christian titles — books from local and international authors, dictionaries, encyclopaedias, Bible commentaries, Bibles, children's Bibles, story books and learning materials for children.

The library also launched the Apostle Akomeah Children's Reading Club on 28 February 2026, to help children in the community with academic learning while nurturing their spiritual growth.

Our identity

Vision & mission

The guiding vision and mission of Jesus Power School of Ministry.

The urge to establish and operate the JPower Coldwater Foundation comes from a unique story — well documented in the book "Product of Grace" — which chronicles a long and laborious walk from rejection, hopelessness and obscurity, without any requisite formal education, into the wonderful service of Jehovah God. The ideals of the Foundation draw strength and ground themselves in Scripture found in Colossians 1:25–29 and Matthew 25:34–40.

Slogan: "Raising Champions to Advance God's Kingdom."
